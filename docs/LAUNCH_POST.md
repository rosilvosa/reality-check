I shipped a web app today because I could not afford to put it on the stores.

**Reality Check** — https://reality-check-ph.web.app

It is a gambling recovery tool for the Philippines. You type a loss. It tells you what that money actually was: hours of your life, weeks of groceries, a month of rent. There is a journal that forces you to re-read the last entry before you write another one. That is the intercept. There is also a near-miss reframe, a house-edge calculator, a barriers checklist (PAGCOR self-exclusion, delete the apps, block the sites), and a streak tracker.

I built it because digital chips do not feel like money. ₱5,000 on a screen feels like nothing. Three weeks of groceries does not.

**Why web, not an app**

Apple is $99/year. Play is a listing + review + privacy labels + a build pipeline I do not have budget for. The people I want to reach are already on a phone browser. “Add to Home Screen” is enough for v1.

**What is free**

Everything. No account required. Data stays in the browser. Sign in with Google if you want the same journal on another device — that backup is also free. I will not charge someone in a binge ₱299 to keep the one record that might stop the next spin.

**How it stays alive**

Optional donations through PayMongo (GCash / Maya / QRPH / card) or Ko-fi. That money keeps Firebase on and funds a safety app I am releasing soon. No features are locked behind it.

**Stack, if you care**

React + Vite + Zustand, Firebase Auth/Firestore/Hosting in Singapore, PayMongo for PH rails. Local-first storage adapter; Firestore only after sign-in. Five languages: English, Filipino, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano.

If you build apps: this is the version I wish I had shipped six months ago. Not the store listing. The thing people can open tonight.

If you gamble and you are trying to stop: open it after a loss, before you chase. That is the whole product.

https://reality-check-ph.web.app
